Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026

Who we are

LabelClair is an iPhone app that explains food labels for your own health. It is published by Melvin Fred Rayan Ouedraogo, Bobo-Dioulasso, Burkina Faso ("we"). We decide how your data is used, so we are the data controller. Contact: appmafia2@icloud.com.

What we collect

  • Account: your e-mail address, or the identifier given by Sign in with Apple or Google.
  • Your health card: what you tell us about yourself: name, age, goals, health conditions, life stage (for example pregnancy), medicines, allergies and notes. This is health data.
  • Label photos: the photos of ingredient lists and nutrition facts you take or pick.
  • Results and questions: the explanations written for you and the questions you ask about a product.
  • Subscription status: whether your subscription is active and when it ends. We never see your card details. Payment is handled by Apple.
  • Usage counter: how many scans you have used this month.

We do not use advertising, ad trackers or analytics tools. We do not collect your location or your contacts.

Microphone and speech

If you choose to talk instead of typing, your voice is turned into text by Apple's speech recognition on your iPhone. We only receive the text, never the recording.

Why we use it, and on what legal basis

  • To run the app and your account (contract).
  • To explain labels for your health: we use your health card, your label photos and your questions. Because this is health data, we only do it with your explicit consent, which you give when you sign up (GDPR Article 9(2)(a)). You can withdraw it at any time in Account → Privacy & health data. Without it, LabelClair cannot explain labels for you.
  • To manage your subscription and monthly scan limit (contract).
  • To keep the service safe and fix problems (legitimate interest).

We never sell your data. We never use it for advertising.

Who processes your data for us

  • Anthropic (Claude AI): reads your label photos, your health card and your questions to write your answers. Anthropic does not use this data to train its AI models. It may keep it for a limited time, under its commercial terms, for safety and abuse monitoring.
  • Supabase: hosts your account, your health card, your results and your photos (servers in the United States).
  • RevenueCat: tracks whether your subscription is active.
  • Apple: payments, Sign in with Apple, speech recognition and notifications. Google: Sign in with Google, if you use it.

Each of them only processes your data on our instructions. Some of them are in the United States. Transfers outside the European Union are covered by the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses or the EU–US Data Privacy Framework.

How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account exists. When you delete your account, your health card, photos, results and questions are deleted from our servers right away. Data held by our providers is removed within their own deadlines, usually 30 days at most.

Your rights

You can, at any time:

  • delete your account and all your data in Account → Delete account;
  • withdraw your consent for health data in Account → Privacy & health data;
  • get a copy of your data, or ask us to correct it: use Ask for a copy of my data in the app, or write to appmafia2@icloud.com;
  • object to some uses, or ask us to limit them.

We answer within one month. If you are not satisfied, you can complain to your data protection authority (in Burkina Faso, the CIL: cil.bf; in France, the CNIL: cnil.fr).

Security

Your data is encrypted in transit. Each person can only access their own data, and photos are stored in a private space.

Children

LabelClair is not meant for people under 16. Do not create an account for a child.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date above and let you know in the app for important changes.
